# 🇸🇬 Singapore — GST

**Level 1 (single rate)** · Last verified: *pending, verify before use* · [Back to index](README.md)

## Summary card

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Tax name | Goods and Services Tax (GST) |
| Authority | Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) |
| Standard rate | **9%** (since 1 January 2024) |
| Other rates | Zero-rated (0%) for exports and international services. A few exempt supplies. |
| Currency | SGD, 2 decimals |
| Usual price display | **Tax-inclusive** for consumers |
| Registration threshold | S$1 million annual taxable turnover |
| Levels of government | One (national) |

## What is taxed at which rate

| Category | Treatment | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Almost all goods and services | **9%** | Including food, medicines, education, clothing, books |
| Exports, international services | **Zero-rated (0%)** | Goods shipped overseas |
| Financial services | **Exempt** | Loan interest, bank fees |
| Residential property sale or rent | **Exempt** | Renting an apartment |
| Investment precious metals, digital payment tokens | **Exempt** | Gold bars, crypto tokens |

## How to calculate

- **Price excludes GST:** `GST = price × 9%`
- **Price includes GST:** `GST = price × 9 ÷ 109`
- Round per line to the cent, half-up (simplified).
- A business customer is still charged GST.

## Worked examples

| # | Situation | Calculation | GST | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Printer S$100.00 (ex GST) | 100.00 × 9% | **S$9.00** | S$109.00 |
| 2 | Same printer, price S$109.00 incl GST | 109.00 × 9 ÷ 109 | **S$9.00** | S$109.00 |
| 3 | Bank fee S$100.00 | Exempt | **S$0.00** | S$100.00 |

## Out of scope for v1

Overseas vendor registration (imported low-value goods and digital services), reverse charge, Major Exporter Scheme, tourist refund scheme, gross margin scheme.
